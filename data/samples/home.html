About Sametham
Sametham is an initiative from KITE to share the authentic data of academic and infrastructural details of Govt, Aided and Recognized UnAided schools in Kerala. Sametham opens the doors to view the basic details of schools including its physical infrastructure, governing mechanisms etc. Details which are available in Sametham include the approved classes in each school, details of students in each class, teachers and non-teaching staff (without personal details) etc which can be used for various report generations at school, educational sub-district, educational district, revenue district and state level.
