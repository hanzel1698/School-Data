# Kerala DGE/KITE — 2026-27 Staff Fixation & Sixth Working Day Circulars

Consolidated summary of 9 Google Drive PDFs + 1 uploaded PDF, all relating to the
Kerala Directorate of General Education (DGE) / KITE **"Sixth Working Day" student
count and teacher post (staff) fixation process for the 2026-27 academic year**.

## Document-by-document summary

### 1. DGE Circular, 01.06.2026 (No. DGE/9368/2025-H2) — *Sixth Working Day data collection*
- School reopened 01.06.2026 → **6th working day = 08.06.2026**
- Sampoorna data entry allowed only **until 5:00 PM, 08.06.2026** (portal freezes after)
- HM must certify the report; chain of submission: HM → DEO/AEO → DDE → DGE Statistics wing
- Language-option data (Arabic/Sanskrit/Urdu/Kannada etc.) must be recorded accurately by 6th day
- Staff fixation based on **UID count on roll as of 6th working day**
- Invalid UIDs must be validated before 6th day; KITE Master Trainers to assist
- TCs (Transfer Certificates) with UID must be issued promptly to avoid double-counting

### 2. DGE Circular, 02.06.2026 — Amendment
- Withdraws the earlier requirement (Para 3 of Circular 1) that HMs collect written parental
  declarations of language choice. Rest of Circular 1 unchanged.

### 3. KPM-6WD-UPDATION-SAMPOORNA (04.06.2026) — Slide/how-to deck
- Step-by-step guide for schools updating Sampoorna data and validating UIDs before the
  5 PM, 08.06.2026 deadline.

### 4. KPM Staff Fixation Circular, 17.06.2026 (DGE/9368/2025-H2)
- Invalid UID validation deadline **extended to 25.06.2026**
- Children with only EID (no UID yet) → time extended to **14.07.2026**
- District-wise staff-fixation proposal confirmation dates in SAMANWAYA:
  - **18.06.2026** – Malappuram, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha
  - **19.06.2026** – Kozhikode, Kannur
  - **20.06.2026** – Palakkad, Wayanad, Thiruvananthapuram
  - **22.06.2026** – Kasaragod, Kottayam, Thrissur
  - **23.06.2026** – Ernakulam, Idukki, Kollam
  - **24.06.2026** – catch-up day for any missed districts
- KITE CEO to sync 6th-working-day Sampoorna counts to SAMANWAYA by 17.06.2026
- Valid UIDs synced before 25.06.2026; late UIDs (up to 14.07.2026) synced same day as validated
- **Teacher Bank list submission deadline: 14.07.2026, 5:00 PM** (EOs → DDEs)
- **DDEs must finalize Teacher Bank by 15.07.2026**
- Excess-post staff (if any) relieved on 14.07.2026 itself

### 5. KITE Circular, 17.06.2026 — UID verification process
- Distinguishes "Present Strength as on Today" vs. official **"Sixth Working Day Strength
  for Staff Fixation/SAMANWAYA"** count (only the latter counts for staff fixation)
- Aadhaar photo upload for invalid UIDs extended to 20.06.2026
- Schools verify validity of uploads by 21.06.2026
- District-level KITE verification team review: 22–23.06.2026
- Unresolved cases → documentary verification at KITE State Office: 24–25.06.2026
- Late UID upload allowed till 14.07.2026 (per Govt Order)

### 6/7/8. KPM-SF Circular, 09.07.2026 (DGE/8895/2026-H2) — Additional instructions
*(Links 7 and 8 point to the same file as 6.)*
- Schools under renovation/construction (Vidyakiranam/plan-fund projects) with provisional
  fitness certificates: previous year's (2025-26) staff fixation may continue for 2026-27,
  provided classes run in fitness-certified buildings only
- Clarifies: **provisional fitness certificates (1–2 months' validity) are NOT valid basis**
  for a full year's staff fixation — a full-year fitness certificate is required
- If fitness certificate unavailable, staff fixation order cannot be issued →
  **salary cannot be released from 15.07.2026** for affected staff; seniority-based
  redeployment required from that date

### 9. DGE Circular, 11.07.2026 (DGE/8895/2026-H2)
- **Withdraws** the instruction issued in the 09.07.2026 circular (#6/7/8). No replacement
  instruction given in this document.

### 10. (Uploaded) Malappuram DDE Deployment Order, 14.07.2026 (No. DDEMPM/7383/2026-A4)
- Downstream implementation of the 14.07.2026 "excess staff relieved" step from Circular 4
- Redeploys **91 named LPST/UPST teachers** (with PEN numbers) from schools that lost posts
  in 2026-27 staff fixation to schools with available posts, based on:
  - Ref 1: DGE circular 17.06.2026
  - Ref 2: District/Sub-district EO reports
- **15.07.2026** — losing-school HMs must relieve these teachers; teachers must join new
  posting the **same day**
- If a listed vacancy turns out unavailable, school must immediately inform DDE office;
  teacher joins new location only after further instructions
- Teachers with **Census duty** must continue fulfilling it per Census Director's orders,
  without delay, despite the transfer
- Posting is **subject to review** once the general transfer process (പൊതു സ്ഥലംമാറ്റം) for
  2026-27 is carried out
- Schools/HMs and teachers must report relieving and joining dates to DDE promptly
